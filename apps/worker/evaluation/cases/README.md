# Synthetic selection cases

40 synthetic text scenes: dev 10, holdout 30, five categories (2/6 per category).
Created by one AI author on 2026-10-20, before evaluation predictions. No expert consensus,
independent human review, real photos or production observations are claimed.

`tools/build_evaluation_cases.py` documents the explicit inputs and labels. Labels are authored
there, not generated by a rules/model prediction. Schema, split, exact-input duplicates and fixed
file hashes are verified before a run. Related policy patterns occur in both splits; group IDs
do not prove semantic independence. Do not regenerate/relabel holdout after inspecting results.

`support_possible` marks an actionable, permitted change with a remaining comparison slot.
Correctness is allowed-set membership, not an attractiveness or image-quality rating.
`forbidden_final_choices` covers explicit restrictions, known unobservable conditions and hard
limits at the four-choice level. Wrong but harmless routing is counted as a mismatch, not
automatically called a safety violation. Wording and concrete physical actions are not evaluated.

These are small purposive cases, not representative independent users. Three repeats of the
30 holdout scenes remain 30 scenes. General model accuracy, Bedrock observation, advice safety,
AWS performance and product usefulness require separate evidence.
